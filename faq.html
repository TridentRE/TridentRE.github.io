Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers about inspections, insurance, service areas, and how Trident approaches roofing and exterior work in Ontario.

Do you offer free roof inspections?

Yes. Trident Roofing & Exteriors provides free, no-obligation inspections and consultations for roofing, siding, skylights, eavestroughs, and complete exterior systems. We assess your home honestly and explain what actually needs attention — without pressure to replace unnecessarily.

What areas do you serve?

We are based in London, Ontario and primarily serve communities within roughly a 2-hour / 200 km drive — including Kitchener-Waterloo, Cambridge, St. Thomas, Woodstock, Guelph, Brantford, and surrounding South and Central Ontario. We also take on select projects elsewhere in Ontario, such as Muskoka cottages. See our full service area list for details.

Can you help with insurance claims for storm damage?

Yes. We regularly assist homeowners with storm and wind damage documentation, scope of work, and coordination with insurance adjusters. We provide clear photos, detailed reports, and professional repair or replacement options that meet insurer requirements.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Trident Roofing & Exteriors is fully licensed and insured, including $5,000,000 liability coverage. We carry WSIB coverage for our crews and follow Ontario building code and manufacturer installation standards on every project.

What brands and materials do you install?

We install premium systems from trusted manufacturers including IKO architectural shingles, Velux skylights and sun tunnels, Alu-Rex gutter guards, and Westlake Royal siding. We are a certified installer for several of these brands, which helps protect your material and workmanship warranties.

Do you offer emergency roof repairs?

Yes. For active leaks or urgent storm damage, call us at (226) 680-0996. We offer 24/7 emergency leak mitigation and temporary protection to prevent further water intrusion until a permanent repair or replacement can be scheduled.

What makes Trident different from other roofing contractors?

We treat your home's exterior as an integrated system — roofing, ventilation, drainage, siding, and flashing designed to work together. With 20+ years of experience, certified brand partnerships, and strong customer reviews, we focus on engineered durability and honest recommendations rather than quick patch jobs or unnecessary upsells.

How do I get a quote?

Contact us through our online form, email info@tridentroofingandexteriors.ca, or call (226) 680-0996. We will schedule a free on-site inspection and provide a detailed written quote for the work discussed.
