Our Process

From first call to final walkthrough — a transparent, no-pressure approach to roofing and exterior work in Ontario.

1. Free inspection & consultation

We start with an on-site visit at no cost. Our team inspects your roof, siding, eavestroughs, or full exterior system — including attic ventilation, flashing, and drainage. You receive clear photos and an honest assessment of what actually needs attention.

2. Detailed proposal

If work is needed, we provide a written scope covering materials, installation methods, timeline, and pricing. No vague estimates — you know exactly what is included before you decide.

3. Engineered installation

Trident crews install to manufacturer specifications and Ontario building code — including proper underlayment, ice-and-water shield, ventilation, and fastening patterns. We treat roofing, siding, and drainage as one integrated system, not isolated trades.

4. Site protection & cleanup

We protect landscaping, use magnetic nail sweeps, and leave your property clean. Debris is hauled away — not left at the curb for you to deal with.

5. Final walkthrough & follow-up

Before we close the job, we walk through the completed work with you. Manufacturer warranty registration and workmanship coverage are documented. If questions come up after installation, we respond.

Storm & insurance projects

For storm damage, we document conditions with photos, provide emergency tarping when needed, and work with your insurer. See our storm damage guide for what to do after severe weather.
