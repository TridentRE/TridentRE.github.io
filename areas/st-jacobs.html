Does a property near the Conestogo River need different shingles?

Shingle selection starts with roof slope, ventilation, shade and the condition of the deck. For a home near the Conestogo River, we review those building details alongside the manufacturer's installation requirements. Can you assess a roof and eavestrough problem on a rural Woolwich property together?
