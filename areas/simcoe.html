Simcoe homeowner questions

Can roof work be planned for a building with a shop and residence?

Tell us how the Simcoe building is used and where access is shared. We can discuss the proposed sequence, inspection access and any temporary protection needed. The scope should identify which roof areas are included and how work may affect occupants. Mention entrances, shared areas and recurring leak locations so those details can be considered together.

Can you check dampness around an upstairs wall or window?

We can review the nearby roof, flashing and siding junctions for visible defects within our exterior scope. Tell us where dampness first appears, whether it follows rain and how long it lasts. Photos taken safely inside can help with that discussion. At your Simcoe property, the findings will guide which repair is recommended and whether further investigation is needed.

What should a Simcoe commercial owner save after storm damage?

Keep safe photographs, the date the damage was noticed and notes about any active water entry. Retain records of temporary protection and previous roof work if available. We can document visible conditions and discuss repairs. Your insurer determines coverage, so our support does not include a promise that particular work will be covered.
