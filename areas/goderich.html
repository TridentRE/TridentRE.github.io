Goderich homeowner questions

Does a Goderich heritage designation affect a roof replacement?

It can affect the material, profile and visible details you propose to change. Goderich has heritage conservation districts as well as individually designated properties. Check your property's status and the applicable municipal guidance before ordering materials. We can describe the roof work clearly so you can confirm whether heritage approval is needed for that scope.

Should a home near Lake Huron automatically get a metal roof?

No single material is right for every shoreline property. We assess the roof's slope, existing deck, ventilation, exposure and appearance before comparing options. Shingles or metal may be suitable depending on those findings. If the building has heritage protection, material changes also need to be considered against the applicable requirements rather than chosen on exposure alone.

Can you inspect gutters and siding during a Goderich roof visit?

The visit can include those concerns. Let us know about overflow, loose siding or damaged fascia when you request the assessment. We can review how those parts meet the roof and identify related work. Combining the assessment helps avoid replacing a gutter before addressing a damaged roof edge. Proposed repairs or replacements will be explained in the scope.
