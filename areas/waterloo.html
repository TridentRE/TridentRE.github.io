Waterloo homeowner questions

Can I change roof materials in the MacGregor-Albert district?

Material changes may need heritage review, so check Waterloo's district plan and confirm the requirements for your property before ordering. The roof's profile and visible edges can be important parts of its appearance. We can set out the proposed covering, flashing and trim details clearly, helping you compare repair options while keeping any approval requirements in view.

Should siding and roof-edge trim be planned together in Waterloo?

Yes, when both need attention. Siding meets soffit, fascia, capping and flashing at several points, and the order of work affects those connections. We can inspect the related components and separate necessary repairs from optional appearance changes. On a heritage property, confirm the acceptable finishes before finalising the combined scope or selecting colours.

Will new eavestroughs solve water beside my Waterloo foundation?

They may help if overflow or poor downspout placement is contributing, but they are not a blanket solution. We check the roof-edge drainage and explain any defects we can identify. Ground slope, underground piping or foundation issues may need separate assessment. Any changed discharge location also needs to respect the property's layout and applicable municipal requirements.
