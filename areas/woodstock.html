Woodstock homeowner questions

How do I plan roofing work on a historic Woodstock property?

First establish whether the property has heritage status and what requirements apply to the intended work. Woodstock provides heritage information and has a heritage advisory committee. We can then discuss the condition of the roof and describe the proposed materials and details. A building's appearance deserves consideration, but it does not replace a roof condition assessment.

Can a downtown business arrange exterior repairs around its entrance?

Tell us how the entrance, deliveries and occupied areas are used when requesting a Woodstock assessment. We can consider those needs when discussing access, material handling and the work area. The roof or wall condition still determines the repair scope. Planning access early helps identify practical constraints before roofing, siding or eavestrough work is scheduled.

Are gutter guards useful if trees overhang my Woodstock roof?

They can help reduce the debris entering eavestroughs, but the type of debris and existing drainage layout matter. We check outlets, trough condition and downspout function before recommending guards. Leaves, twigs and smaller particles can behave differently around a guard. Periodic inspection and cleaning remain important, including checks that water reaches the outlets during rain.
