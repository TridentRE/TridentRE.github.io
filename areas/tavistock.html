Tavistock homeowner questions

Can loose fascia be repaired during a Tavistock roofing job?

Yes, fascia can be assessed with the roof edge and included in the proposed work where needed. We check whether the visible damage involves only the finish or also the supporting material. That distinction matters before reattaching gutters or adding capping, since a new covering needs a sound base beneath it.

Can you inspect a garage roof separately from my Tavistock house?

Identify the building when requesting service and describe its roof covering and the concern you have noticed. A garage and a house can have different slopes, ventilation and repair needs. We can discuss them separately or in a coordinated scope, depending on their condition, rather than assuming one replacement decision applies to both.

What should I send before a Tavistock exterior inspection?

Provide the complete address, a description of the affected building and safe photographs showing the problem and access. Mention gates, limited turning space or any restrictions that could affect ladders and deliveries. Those details help us prepare. They do not replace an on-site assessment of the roof, wall or eavestrough needing attention.
