Exeter homeowner questions

Could an Exeter water stain come from something other than the roof?

Yes. The stain's location, when it appears and what lies above it all matter. Roof leaks, condensation and other moisture sources can look similar indoors. Record whether it follows rain or snowmelt and take photos if safe. We assess the roofing details within our scope and explain when the evidence points to a different issue needing investigation.

Can you assess eavestrough problems during an Exeter roof visit?

Yes, mention overflowing gutters, dripping joints or water collecting near the walls when you book. We can look at the roof's path to the eavestroughs and the condition of outlets and downspouts. The proposal can distinguish roof repairs from drainage work, including any ground-level issue that falls outside our roofing and eavestrough scope.

Can storm repairs in Exeter be documented for an insurance claim?

We can assess visible roof and exterior damage, record findings and describe the recommended repair scope. If safe, keep your own dated photos and note when you first noticed the issue. Your insurer decides coverage and claim requirements. We can support the process with information about the work, but cannot promise approval or a particular claim outcome.
