Chatham homeowner questions

Will new eavestroughs solve water problems near the Thames River?

New eavestroughs can improve the collection of roof water when the existing system is defective. They are not a remedy for river flooding, groundwater or every grading problem. We inspect the roof drainage and explain what that work can address. If water has several possible sources, those need to be distinguished before a replacement is recommended.

Can you plan roofing around an occupied Chatham business?

We can discuss commercial roofing with access and business activity in mind. Let us know about entrances, deliveries, parking, tenants and any sensitive indoor areas when requesting an assessment. The proposal can then identify the roof work and coordination it requires. Scheduling and site arrangements are agreed for the property rather than promised before the roof is inspected.

What if storm damage affects both my Chatham roof and siding?

Ask for the affected roof and wall areas to be reviewed together. Loose siding, damaged fascia and displaced roofing may require connected repairs at their junctions. We can record visible damage and help you understand the repair scope for an insurance enquiry. Your insurer determines coverage, and inspection findings determine whether local repairs or larger replacements are appropriate.
