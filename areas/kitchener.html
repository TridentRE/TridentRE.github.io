Kitchener homeowner questions

Can roof and siding work be phased on a Kitchener property?

Yes, provided the connections between phases are planned. We look at roof-to-wall flashing, capping, fascia and gutter attachments before recommending an order. Some details are easier to complete while adjacent materials are removed. For a designated property, confirm any required heritage approval for the full proposal, even if the installation will happen in separate stages.

What changes need extra thought in Victoria Park or Upper Doon?

Visible roof shapes, materials and exterior details can contribute to a district's character. Kitchener has guidance for its heritage districts and a permit process for applicable alterations. Before changing shingles to metal or replacing siding and trim with a different profile, check the property's designation and the relevant guidance. We can help define the proposed physical changes.

Should gutters come off when replacing a Kitchener roof?

Not automatically. Their condition, position and attachment matter, along with the roof edge work being proposed. We inspect the gutters and fascia to see whether they can remain, need adjustment or should be replaced. If you plan new soffit or fascia as well, discussing it early helps establish an order that avoids unnecessary removal of completed work.
