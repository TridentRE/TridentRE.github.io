Norwich homeowner questions

Should a Norwich house and detached workshop use the same roofing material?

Not necessarily: their slopes, existing coverings and uses may call for different solutions. We can review both roofs and compare suitable shingle, metal or flat roofing options. Matching appearances can be part of the discussion, but the preparation, flashing and drainage plan should suit each building. Tell us whether either structure has recurring leaks or previous repairs.

Can roof work be planned around an active rural property?

We can discuss access and the order of work before setting the scope. Tell us about vehicle movements, animals, restricted areas and the buildings that need attention. On a Norwich-area site, those practical details can affect material placement and cleanup. The plan should keep the work area separate from other activities wherever possible.

What should I check before changing a historic building's roof in Norwich?

Gather any information about the property's designation, previous roof materials and distinctive trim. Photographs of earlier work can help explain which features you want to retain. We can assess the physical roof and discuss suitable options that respect those details. Required municipal approvals should be settled before ordering materials or changing protected features.
