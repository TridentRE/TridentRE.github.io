Paris homeowner questions

How do you assess a leak where a roof meets cobblestone or brick?

We examine the roof-to-wall connection, visible flashing and nearby roof materials to look for a likely entry route. Water can travel before it appears indoors, so a stain alone is not a diagnosis. For a Paris heritage property, the repair plan should also consider the masonry and any restrictions on altering protected exterior details.

Should I check heritage status before choosing new shingles in Paris?

Yes, confirm whether your property is designated and whether the proposed work needs approval. Paris contains recognised heritage buildings, but the rules are not identical for every address. We can explain roofing condition and suitable material options during an assessment. Settle any required municipal review before making a final choice or placing a material order.

Can you repair eavestroughs while protecting a Paris home's masonry?

We can assess leaking joints, overflow and downspout placement alongside the fascia and nearby masonry. The aim is to collect roof runoff and keep it from spilling repeatedly onto the wall. Loose supports or damaged fascia may need attention before new gutters are installed. Tell us about staining or damp patches, and identify any protected exterior features before work is planned.
