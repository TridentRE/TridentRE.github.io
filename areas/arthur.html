Arthur homeowner questions

Can you assess a house and a separate garage on an Arthur property?

Yes. Let us know about both buildings when requesting the visit. We can review each roof separately and explain whether their materials, condition or drainage call for different work. Include photos taken from the ground if possible. Separate scopes make it easier to decide what needs attention now and what can be planned later.

What should I note before a roof leak inspection in Arthur?

Note the room affected, when the stain first appeared and whether it grows during rain or snowmelt. Photos taken indoors and safely from the ground can help us follow the water's path. We examine flashings, penetrations, shingles and roof edges, then explain the likely repair scope and any further checks needed before work begins.

How can Arthur homeowners combine roofing and exterior work?

Start by listing the problems rather than choosing products immediately. A leaking roof, stained soffit and overflowing eavestrough may involve connected details. We can quote roofing, siding, fascia and drainage work together or identify sensible stages. The goal is to avoid finishing one surface only to disturb it during the next part of the project.
