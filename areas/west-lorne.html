West Lorne homeowner questions

What should I check after wind lifts shingles near West Lorne?

From the ground, note any missing shingles, loose metal edges or pieces in the yard, and look indoors for new staining. Keep clear of loose materials and avoid climbing onto the roof. Tell us when the damage appeared and which building is affected. We can assess the covering and nearby flashings before explaining the repair options.

Can you assess a house and a separate garage roof together?

We can discuss both buildings in one enquiry for a West Lorne property. Each roof still needs its own assessment because pitch, condition and drainage may differ. Send photos of each building if available and identify any active leak. The proposed scope can then distinguish immediate repairs from replacement work that can be planned separately.

Can siding repairs include the soffit and fascia on my West Lorne home?

Yes, those connections are worth inspecting when siding is loose, stained or due for replacement. We look at how the wall finish meets the roofline and whether trim or flashing needs repair. The scope can include soffit, fascia and capping where needed, while identifying sound components that can stay in place.
