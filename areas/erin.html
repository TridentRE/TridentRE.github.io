Erin homeowner questions

What roof maintenance matters on a wooded Erin property?

Keep an eye on leaves collecting in valleys, blocked gutter outlets and branches close to the roof. Ground-level photos can help show recurring trouble spots without climbing. We can review the affected roofing and drainage, then discuss repairs or gutter guard options. Any tree work needs to be planned separately from the agreed exterior repair scope.

How should roof drainage work on a sloping Erin lot?

The eavestrough needs to collect runoff, but the downspout also needs a suitable discharge route. We look at the existing outlets and visible conditions around the building. Ground drainage issues may require work beyond the roofing scope. We will distinguish that from gutter repair rather than suggest a new eavestrough alone can solve every water problem.

Are metal roofs the only choice for rural Erin homes?

No. A rural location does not dictate the roof covering. Roof slope, existing construction, appearance and maintenance preferences all help shape the choice. We can compare metal roofing with IKO or GAF shingles where the roof is suitable. Flat sections need their own assessment, and any visible defect should be addressed rather than covered by a material change.
