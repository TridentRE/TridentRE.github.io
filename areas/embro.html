Embro homeowner questions

Can you assess the house and a detached building near Embro together?

Yes, provided you identify both buildings when arranging the visit. Their roof pitches, materials and uses may differ, so one recommendation will not necessarily fit both. Photos and approximate building descriptions help us prepare. We can discuss separate scopes and priorities, including which roof has active water entry and which needs planning rather than immediate work.

Does living near Embro Pond mean I need gutter guards?

Not automatically. The useful question is how much debris reaches your particular eavestroughs and whether the existing system drains correctly. Nearby branches, roof shape and downspout condition all matter. We can assess gutter guards alongside cleaning and drainage needs, but guards do not remove the need to inspect gutters or correct a poorly positioned outlet.

Can I repair the roof now and replace siding later?

Often, yes. We first check whether the work meets at a wall, fascia or another shared detail. If it does, the repair should account for the later siding project so materials are not needlessly disturbed twice. Tell us your longer-term plans at the inspection, even if you only want the current leak addressed in the first scope.
