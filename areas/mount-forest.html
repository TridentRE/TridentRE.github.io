Mount Forest homeowner questions

Can several roofs on a Wellington North property be reviewed together?

Yes, describe the house, shop or other structures you want included when you contact us. Different roof coverings may need different repairs or replacement plans. We can discuss how to group the assessment and identify access needs. Each building's condition and use should guide the recommendation rather than treating the entire property as one roof.

Does Mount Forest's location mean I should choose metal roofing?

Location alone is not enough to choose a roof material. Slope, the existing structure, drainage details, appearance and maintenance preferences all matter. We can compare suitable metal roofing with IKO or GAF shingles for your property. Any proposal should explain the preparation and flashing work, not just the visible material you will see from the street.

What maintenance makes sense for a roof near the Saugeen Trail?

If branches overhang the roof, arrange safe checks for leaves or twigs in valleys and gutters, and watch for overflow from the ground. Pay attention to outlets after leaves have fallen and before freezing weather arrives. Report recurring dampness, missing materials or drainage changes so we can assess whether cleaning, repair or further investigation is needed.
