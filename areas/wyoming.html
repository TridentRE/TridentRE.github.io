Wyoming homeowner questions

How do you compare shingle and metal roofing for a Wyoming home?

We start with the roof's slope, existing layers, flashing details and your plans for maintenance. Shingles and metal have different installation requirements, so the comparison should cover the full roof system rather than the surface alone. We explain suitable options after assessing the building, including how edges, penetrations and adjoining roof sections would be finished.

Can you combine work on my Wyoming house and business exterior?

You can include both properties in an enquiry, with a separate address and description for each. Trident provides residential and commercial roofing as well as siding and roofline work. Each building needs an appropriate assessment and a clear scope. Let us know about occupied areas, entrances and active leaks so those practical needs are considered from the start.

What should I send about storm damage to a Wyoming roof?

Send the property address, when you noticed the damage and any photos taken safely from the ground. Describe interior water entry as well as missing or displaced materials. Do not climb onto a damaged roof for pictures. We can assess visible roofing and exterior damage and provide documentation, while your insurer decides the terms and outcome of a claim.
