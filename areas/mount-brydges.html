Should I replace fascia before ordering new eavestroughs?

The fascia should be checked first, especially if it is soft, stained or pulling away. New eavestroughs need sound support and a planned route for runoff. On a Mount Brydges exterior update, we can assess fascia, soffit and roof edges together. That allows any necessary repairs to be considered before the new drainage is installed.

What if my property has details featured in the village's heritage character?

Photograph the details you want to preserve and gather any information about the property's heritage status. We can discuss how roofing, capping or siding choices might affect their appearance. Requirements are specific to the property and proposed work. Confirm applicable municipal approvals before committing to changes that could alter protected exterior features.
