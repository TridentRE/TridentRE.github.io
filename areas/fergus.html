Fergus homeowner questions

Can new shingles fix a leak where a Fergus stone wall meets the roof?

Only if the complete source of the leak is addressed. The roof covering, wall condition and flashing connection need to be considered together. Replacing shingles while leaving a failed junction untouched may leave the problem in place. We assess that detail, explain the proposed roofing repair and identify any related work outside the agreed scope.

What if my Fergus house has an addition with a different roof?

Mention the addition when booking, particularly if the leak is near the join. Different roof pitches and coverings may need separate details, and water can move from one section to another. We can assess the connection and discuss whether the work should cover one section or several. A full replacement is not assumed simply because the materials differ.

Do all properties in the Fergus historic core need heritage approval?

Requirements depend on the building's heritage status and the alteration you are planning. Check Centre Wellington's records and confirm the process before ordering a different visible roof or exterior finish. With those details settled, we can discuss materials and define a repair or replacement scope that accounts for the protected features.
