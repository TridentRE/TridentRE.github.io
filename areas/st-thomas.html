St. Thomas homeowner questions

Can a downtown St. Thomas building receive new siding?

That depends on the property and the proposed work. Parts of downtown are within a Heritage Conservation District, so check the applicable rules before selecting a different cladding material. We can assess the wall condition and describe repair or replacement options. Any required municipal review should be settled before materials are ordered or existing surfaces removed.

Why is water staining the trim below my St. Thomas roof?

Water marks below an eave can come from overflowing gutters, a loose roof-edge detail or leakage behind the fascia. We inspect the connection before suggesting new trim or capping. Covering the stain alone may hide continuing damage, so the repair should address the water path and any affected support as well as the finish.

Can you separate storm repairs from a planned St. Thomas exterior update?

We can distinguish damage needing prompt attention from optional work such as a siding change or new gutter guards. Photographs and a clear repair scope can support your conversation with an insurer. Coverage decisions belong to the insurer, so keep records and confirm its process before disposing of damaged material when it is safe to retain it.
