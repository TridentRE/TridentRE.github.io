Seaforth homeowner questions

Does Seaforth's heritage district affect every roof replacement?

The district covers a defined area, not the whole community. Requirements also depend on the property and the proposed changes. Before work begins, establish whether your building is designated or within the district and what approvals apply. We can describe the roofing scope and materials so you have clear details for that review.

Can you look at a flat roof behind a Main Street facade?

A street-facing facade may conceal roof surfaces and drainage points that need their own assessment. We can review the flat roof, wall flashings, penetrations and outlets, subject to safe access. For a Seaforth commercial building, mention any shared access, occupants and recurring leak locations when arranging the visit so we can plan accordingly.

Can original exterior trim stay while damaged roofing is removed?

We first assess its condition and how it connects to the roof edge. Retaining sound trim may be possible, but concealed damage or the required flashing detail can affect the plan. On a Seaforth property with protected features, the agreed approach should also respect applicable approvals. We explain those interfaces before the scope is finalised.
