Burford homeowner questions

Can you plan roof work around a Burford building with several uses?

We can discuss access and staging when assessing the roof. Tell us about occupied areas, public entrances and any restrictions on deliveries or work zones. Those details help shape the scope and sequence. Before work starts, we agree on access arrangements and identify the adjoining surfaces that need protection during removal and installation.

Can exterior repairs retain a Burford building's historic details?

We can assess the roof, siding and trim together to identify what needs repair and which details can remain. Connections around existing features need careful planning so new work provides weather protection without unnecessary removal. If the property has heritage requirements, confirm those before making changes. Our scope can describe the proposed materials and affected areas.

What if a leak starts where two roof sections meet?

A junction can involve flashing, drainage and materials with different slopes or service lives. We inspect the connection rather than assume both roof sections need replacement. Share the indoor leak location and any previous repair history. For a Burford property with additions or adjoining roof areas, that information helps us focus the inspection without jumping to a conclusion.
