Port Dover homeowner questions

Does a roof near Port Dover's waterfront have to be metal?

Metal and shingles can both be worth considering, depending on the roof. Pitch, the supporting surface, existing details and the product's installation requirements all matter. We can compare suitable options after seeing the building, including how the covering connects at edges and penetrations. That gives you a practical basis for choosing a system for your Port Dover property.

Can you assess a Port Dover property while I am away?

Contact us with the property details and the concerns you want checked. We can discuss access arrangements and whether someone needs to be present before confirming an appointment. Interior leaks may require access inside. Clear photos can help us prepare, but they do not replace an on-site assessment of concealed or hard-to-see conditions.

What if water appears where my lower roof meets the main building?

That junction deserves a close look at flashing, roof covering and the adjoining wall. Water may travel before it becomes visible indoors, so the stain alone does not identify its source. At your Port Dover property, we can assess that transition and explain whether a targeted repair or more extensive work is appropriate.
