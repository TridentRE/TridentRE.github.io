Waterford homeowner questions

Do homes near Waterford's ponds need a different roof?

The best choice depends on roof slope, the existing structure and how you want to maintain it. For a pond-side property, we also look at nearby trees and exposed edges when discussing upkeep. Shingles, metal and flat roofing each have suitable applications. An inspection helps narrow the choices and identify the flashing and drainage details needed.

Could I replace my roof and eavestroughs in one Waterford project?

That can be a sensible way to address connected roof-edge problems. We check the fascia, drip edge, soffit and existing drainage before defining the work. If some components remain serviceable, we can discuss keeping them. Coordinating the scope helps avoid fitting new eavestroughs to trim that already needs repair or replacement.

Who should I check with before changing a heritage property's exterior?

Confirm the property's heritage status and any approval requirements with Norfolk County before selecting a different roof covering, siding profile or trim detail. Keeping original proportions or decorative features may be part of the plan. Trident can outline the proposed materials and repairs so you have a clear description to use when checking what is permitted.
