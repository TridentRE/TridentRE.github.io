Wallaceburg homeowner questions

Where do you start when tracing water entry in a Wallaceburg home?

The location and timing of the water help guide the inspection. We check relevant roof details, eavestroughs and wall flashings, and ask whether the problem follows rainfall or melting snow. Photos of interior staining can help connect the symptoms to the exterior. If the source falls outside our roofing and exterior work, we explain that clearly.

Can you assess a downtown shop roof as well as a house?

Trident assesses downtown commercial roofs as well as residential roofing in Wallaceburg. Let us know how the roof is accessed, whether anyone occupies the space below and where water has appeared. We can assess flat or sloped roofing and discuss repairs, replacement and drainage with a scope suited to the building's layout and condition.

What if my Wallaceburg property has heritage status?

Check the property's listing or designation with Chatham-Kent before committing to visible changes. The municipality maintains heritage information, including Wallaceburg properties. We can describe proposed roofing, siding and trim work clearly so you can confirm any requirements. Material selection should follow that check, especially where roof edges or decorative exterior details would change.
