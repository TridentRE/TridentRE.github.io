Stratford homeowner questions

Does Stratford require a heritage permit for repair work as well as replacement?

Repair work can fall within Stratford's heritage permit requirements, as well as replacement. For a designated building or one inside the Heritage Conservation District, check the requirements for the intended scope before starting. We can describe the affected roof sections, proposed materials and visible changes to help you prepare for that review.

Can I compare shingles and metal before deciding on a Stratford roof?

Yes, but first establish the roof's pitch, condition and any heritage restrictions affecting the property. We can then discuss suitable shingle and metal options, including how each would meet walls, valleys and roof edges. A material comparison is most useful when it includes the complete installation scope rather than the visible covering alone.

What information helps when booking an inspection in the City Centre Core?

Tell us the address, the problem and whether you know the property's heritage status. Include any relevant approval documents, safe photographs and details about shared access or occupied commercial space. This helps frame the inspection and the possible scope. We still need to assess the roof itself before recommending repair or replacement.
