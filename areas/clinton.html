Clinton homeowner questions

Can you assess a home and a separate building outside Clinton together?

Yes, we can discuss both in the enquiry. Describe each building, the type of roof if known and the faults you have seen. Photos help distinguish the structures and their access. Each roof needs its own assessment, and the proposal should identify the work included for each rather than treating the entire property as one uniform surface.

What should I do about loose siding after a storm in Clinton?

Keep clear of panels or trim that could fall, and take photos from a safe position on the ground. Note any new indoor dampness and whether nearby fascia or roofing also looks disturbed. We can assess the connected areas and document visible damage. The repair scope depends on the condition of the panels, fastenings and adjoining finishes.

Should I replace the eavestroughs when reroofing my Clinton property?

A roof replacement does not always require new eavestroughs. Sound gutters may be able to stay, while loose fascia, poor slope or damaged outlets can make related work worth addressing during the roof project. We inspect those connections and explain the findings. Combining necessary work can give you a clearer scope without replacing components that still function properly.
