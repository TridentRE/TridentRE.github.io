Guelph homeowner questions

Can a Guelph siding project include the surrounding trim?

Yes, soffit, fascia and capping can be included with the siding scope. We examine how the new finish will meet windows, doors and the roof before recommending which pieces to retain or replace. For a heritage property, visible profiles and materials also need to fit the applicable guidance. Planning these connections early helps avoid disturbing newly finished cladding later.

Can a Brooklyn and College Hill roof keep its existing appearance?

Keeping the roof's appearance is a useful starting point, but the right scope depends on its condition and the applicable district guidance. We can assess repair options, visible roof details and potential replacement materials. Do not assume that a product with a similar colour alone meets heritage expectations. Confirm the proposed change with the City first.

What causes water to spill behind a Guelph gutter?

Possible causes include a loose gutter, damaged roof edge flashing or water backing up at a blockage. We inspect the attachment, fascia and drainage path to narrow down the source. Tell us whether the spill happens along the full run or only at one spot. The findings determine whether cleaning, adjustment, a flashing repair or gutter replacement belongs in the proposal.
