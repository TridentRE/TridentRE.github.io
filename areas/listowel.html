Listowel homeowner questions

Can you repair one leaking section of a Listowel roof?

A targeted repair may be suitable when the failed detail can be isolated and the surrounding roof remains serviceable. We inspect the covering, connections and drainage before recommending that approach. You will receive an explanation of the repair area and its limits. If deterioration extends beyond that section, we can compare a wider replacement scope with the available repair options.

Do you assess flat roofs on downtown Listowel businesses?

Commercial flat roof assessment is part of our service. We review the surface, flashings, penetrations and drainage before explaining repair or replacement options. Tell us about leaks, rooftop access and activity beneath the work area. North Perth's downtown design guidance may be relevant if the project changes visible exterior details rather than only maintaining the existing roof.

What information helps you plan a Listowel roof assessment?

Start with the building type, the leak location and when the problem appears. Mention whether the roof is pitched or flat, whether access is shared and whether a business will remain open. Photographs taken safely from inside or at ground level can help. You do not need to climb onto the roof or identify the failed material yourself.
