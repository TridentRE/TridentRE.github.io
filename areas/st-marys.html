St. Marys homeowner questions

Will a St. Marys heritage property need approval for a roof change?

It may. The town manages alterations to designated properties through its heritage permit process. Establish the property's status and the proposed change before ordering materials or arranging removal. A roof repair and a change in material may raise different questions. We can describe the intended work, but municipal approval is separate from our assessment.

What should I do about a leak where the roof meets limestone?

Note when the water appears and photograph the inside damage from a safe position. Do not climb up or apply a surface coating over the joint yourself. The cause may involve flashing, the roof covering or a masonry issue. We inspect the roof connection and explain which parts fall within the recommended roofing work.

Can new eavestroughs be fitted without replacing a Stonetown home's roof?

Often they can, provided the roof edge and supporting fascia are sound. We check those areas before recommending separate eavestrough work. On a protected property, the appearance of gutters, downspouts and trim may also need review. The aim is to improve water collection without overlooking deterioration or a requirement that affects the installation.
