Tillsonburg homeowner questions

Does a home near Lake Lisgar need a special roofing material?

For a home near Lake Lisgar, the material choice depends on roof slope, ventilation, tree cover and the condition of the existing structure. Shingles, metal and flat roof products suit different applications. We inspect the building and discuss how the complete roof system will handle rain, snowmelt and any debris from overhanging trees.

Would gutter guards solve leaf build-up on a Tillsonburg roof?

Guards address debris entering the eavestrough, not leaves caught higher up in a valley or behind a roof feature. We inspect where material collects and whether the gutters drain properly before recommending them. For a property with overhanging trees, a maintenance plan may still be needed even after suitable guards are installed.

Can a skylight be reviewed during a Tillsonburg reroofing project?

Tell us about condensation, staining or previous leaks around the skylight before the roof assessment. We examine the visible connection and discuss whether it should remain, be repaired or be replaced with a suitable Velux option. Planning that decision with the roofing work helps avoid leaving a questionable detail inside an otherwise renewed roof.
