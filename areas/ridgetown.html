Ridgetown homeowner questions

Can you assess a Ridgetown roof with several additions?

Different roof sections may have different materials, slopes and repair histories. We examine the connections as well as the individual coverings, because a transition can leak even when the adjoining surfaces look sound. Explain any previous additions or recurring trouble when booking so those details can be included in the assessment.

Is metal roofing automatically better for a property outside town?

An open setting is only one consideration. Roof shape, substrate condition, ventilation and the product's installation requirements also influence the choice. For properties around Ridgetown's agricultural surroundings, we can discuss metal and other suitable options after inspection. The comparison includes the roof's exposed edges, existing structure and the work needed to prepare for the selected covering.

Can roof and siding work be planned as separate stages?

They can, provided the order of work protects the junctions between them. At a Ridgetown property, we can identify which flashing, fascia or capping details affect both jobs. The plan should state what happens now, what remains for later and whether any temporary protection is needed between the stages of the work.
