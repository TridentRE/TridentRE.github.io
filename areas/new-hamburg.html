New Hamburg homeowner questions

Is every New Hamburg roof subject to heritage restrictions?

The heritage district has defined boundaries, so its requirements do not automatically apply to every New Hamburg property. Individual buildings may also have their own status. Check the address and proposed work with Wilmot before finalising materials or exterior changes. During an assessment, we can discuss repair needs and the visible details that may be affected.

Can a heritage-sensitive repair avoid replacing the entire roof?

Sometimes a limited repair is reasonable, depending on material condition, the source of the leak and what can be matched. We inspect the affected area and explain the limitations of a repair. On a property in New Hamburg's heritage district, preserving appearance also needs to be considered alongside the applicable municipal requirements and the roof's remaining condition.

What does an eavestrough assessment cover at a Nith-side home?

We look at roof area, gutter condition, slope and downspout discharge, then check for leaks, sagging or signs of overflow. Tell us where runoff collects during rain and whether the problem changes with leaf buildup or winter ice. The aim is reliable roof drainage at the building; river flooding requires a separate property-specific assessment.
