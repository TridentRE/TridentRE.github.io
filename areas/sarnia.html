Sarnia homeowner questions

Will gutter guards solve overflow under Sarnia's trees?

They may help with debris, but they cannot correct every drainage problem. A poorly aligned gutter, restricted downspout or damaged roof edge can still cause overflow. We assess the cause before recommending guards or replacement eavestroughs. On a tree-covered Sarnia property, periodic checks remain important even after guards have been fitted.

Can you match siding while repairing only one wall?

We can review the existing profile and colour and discuss available options. An exact match is not always possible, especially when older material has faded or is no longer available. For your Sarnia property, we explain the likely visual difference before you decide between a limited repair and replacing a larger section.

Who handles a boulevard branch hanging over my roof?

Sarnia manages trees on City property, including boulevard trees. Establish whether the tree is City-owned before arranging pruning. We can explain roof access concerns and document visible roof damage, but roofing work does not replace the tree owner's permission. If the tree is private, responsibility and any necessary approvals still need to be established.
