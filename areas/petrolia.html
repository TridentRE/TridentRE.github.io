Should I replace the gutters when I replace my roof?

Replacement depends on the existing eavestroughs' condition, alignment and how they receive water from the roof. Sound gutters may stay, while damaged fascia or awkward roof-edge details may justify coordinated work. For a Petrolia property with several exterior concerns, a combined assessment helps distinguish necessary repairs from optional changes.

What information helps with a leak at my Petrolia building?
