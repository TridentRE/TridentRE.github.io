Aylmer homeowner questions

Is overflowing guttering beside an Aylmer home always a roofing problem?

Not necessarily. A blockage, poor slope, a restricted outlet or a loose gutter connection can cause overflow even when the roof covering is sound. We inspect the roof edge and drainage together before recommending work. If you can safely photograph the overflow from the ground during rain, that can help show where the problem starts.

Can you investigate a stain that appears when snow thaws?

Yes. Tell us when the stain appeared and whether it changes during rainfall as well. We can check for roof defects, ice dam signs and ventilation concerns, rather than assuming one cause. Ice dam removal or heat cables may be considered where appropriate, but neither replaces finding and addressing the underlying water entry point.

Do you cover exterior repairs beyond Aylmer's town centre?

We serve Aylmer and surrounding properties from our London base. Send the property location, a short description and any safe photos so we can confirm the scope and arrange access.
