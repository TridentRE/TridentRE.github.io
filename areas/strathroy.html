Strathroy homeowner questions

Does living near Strathroy Conservation Area mean I need gutter guards?

Guards may be useful if leaves or twigs repeatedly block your gutters. We check tree overhang, the type of debris and the condition of the existing eavestroughs before recommending a system. A suitable guard can reduce debris entry, but the gutters still need sound attachments, working outlets and periodic inspection to drain properly.

Can you tell roof leakage from a drainage problem beside the Sydenham?

We can inspect the roofing and exterior drainage for defects that may explain water entering the building. River flooding, groundwater and broader site drainage are separate issues and may need a different assessment. Record where the water first appears and the weather at the time. That evidence helps avoid treating unrelated problems as one roof repair.

Should a Strathroy roof replacement include the lower porch roof?

Only if its condition or its connection to the main roof makes that sensible. We inspect the junction, flashing and drainage before recommending a combined scope. A sound lower roof may remain, while a failing transition needs attention. The assessment should make that distinction clear rather than automatically replacing every roof section together.
