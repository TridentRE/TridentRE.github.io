Delhi homeowner questions

Is metal roofing the automatic choice for a Delhi property?

Metal can be a suitable option, but the choice starts with the roof's slope, use and existing condition. Edges, openings and connections to adjoining sections also affect the specification. We can discuss metal alongside shingles or flat roofing where each is suitable, explaining the proposed materials and preparation so you can make an informed choice for your building.

Can you quote separate roof sections at my Norfolk County property?

We can identify separate sections in an assessment and explain which ones need work. Send photos that show how the roofs connect, and mention any lower addition, porch or separate structure. Different sections may need different materials or repair methods. A clear proposal should show the boundaries of the work and any junctions that must be addressed together.

Could new siding and roof-edge work be planned together in Delhi?

They can be reviewed as a coordinated exterior project. Fascia, soffit, capping and wall flashings often meet at points that deserve attention before new siding is fitted. We can discuss Westlake Royal or Royal Building Solutions siding and Green Metal steel options. The final scope depends on the existing condition and any property-specific requirements that apply.
