Caledonia homeowner questions

What should I check when a ceiling stain appears in my Caledonia home?

Note when the stain appears, whether it grows during rain or snowmelt, and whether nearby walls are affected. Possible causes include damaged roofing, failed flashing or condensation. Photograph the mark from indoors and leave roof access to a professional. Those observations help us trace the water entry and recommend the right repair.

Can you combine roof-edge repairs with new siding in Caledonia?

The roof edge, fascia, soffit, wall cladding and eavestroughs can be reviewed in a coordinated scope. This helps identify where flashings and trims must connect before materials are ordered. You do not have to replace every component. We will distinguish the work needed to address the fault from optional changes to the appearance.

What should I send when requesting urgent help at my Caledonia property?

Send the property location, a brief description of the leak and any photos you can take safely from inside or ground level. Mention active dripping or loose material that could fall. We respond to emergency leak enquiries and take some after-hours calls, but availability and weather affect attendance. Do not climb onto a wet or damaged roof.
