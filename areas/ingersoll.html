Ingersoll homeowner questions

Can an Ingersoll shop stay open during roof work?

That depends on the roof, the work area and how the premises are used. Before agreeing on the scope, we discuss entrances, access, falling-debris protection and any activities beneath the roof. Tell us about opening hours and shared areas. We can then explain the proposed work sequence and identify any restrictions rather than promise uninterrupted operation.

Should I check Ingersoll's heritage register before replacing shingles?

It is sensible to check, especially if the building has known heritage features. The Town's register includes different types of properties, so being listed does not by itself explain every requirement. Confirm the property's status and the rules for your specific proposal. We can provide a description of the intended roofing materials and visible changes.

What details help with an Ingersoll roof leak assessment?

Note when the leak first appeared, which room is affected and whether it follows rainfall or melting snow. Photos of the interior stain and any visible exterior damage can help us plan the visit. Stay on the ground when taking outside pictures. We trace likely entry points at the roof, since water can travel before it becomes visible indoors.
