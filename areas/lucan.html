Lucan homeowner questions

Does a newer Lucan home still need a roof inspection?

Yes, a newer roof can still develop a problem at a vent, flashing or roof edge. Arrange a check if you notice a stain, loose material or recurring overflow, even if the covering looks new. An inspection can help separate an isolated defect from wider wear. Keep any installation records or warranty paperwork available when discussing the issue.

Can you combine siding and drainage work on a rural property?

We can assess siding, soffit, fascia and eavestroughs together, including on properties outside the village. The scope depends on wall condition, roof edges and where downspouts discharge. Tell us about any outbuildings you want included. Planning these connections together can avoid finishing one surface before an adjacent repair is addressed.

What should I send when reporting an active leak near Lucan?

Send your location, a description of when the leak started and clear photographs from a safe place. Mention whether water is near electrical fixtures, and keep away from affected electrical areas. We can discuss emergency leak response, including some after-hours calls. Availability and the immediate work possible depend on conditions and safe access.
