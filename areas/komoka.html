Komoka homeowner questions

Do homes near Komoka's wooded areas need gutter guards?

That depends on the trees around the house, the debris reaching the roof and the condition of the existing gutters. We check those factors before recommending guards. They can help limit debris entering the trough, but they still need inspection. A shaded or wooded setting alone does not tell us which guard, if any, suits the property.

Can a Komoka roof leak come from the skylight flashing?

Yes, although flashing is only one possibility. We assess the skylight, nearby roof covering, joints and drainage paths rather than assuming the whole unit must be replaced. Tell us whether water appears during rain or melting snow. If replacement is appropriate, we can discuss a Velux skylight and how it will connect to the surrounding roof.

Will replacing eavestroughs solve a wet yard in Komoka?

Only if faulty roof drainage is contributing to that particular problem. Overflow, a disconnected downspout or discharge beside a foundation can be assessed during our visit. Ground conditions, grading and other site drainage issues are separate matters. We will explain what the gutter work is intended to address without claiming it can correct every source of standing water.
