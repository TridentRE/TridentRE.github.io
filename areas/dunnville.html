Dunnville homeowner questions

Can a Dunnville roof leak be mistaken for a wider drainage problem?

Yes, visible water does not always reveal its source. A roof leak, wall flashing fault, blocked gutter or ground-level issue can require different work. Tell us where the water appears and what conditions bring it on. We assess the roof and exterior details within our scope and explain if the symptoms point to another issue that needs separate investigation.

What if my Dunnville roof only leaks when snow melts?

Mention that pattern when booking an assessment. Melt-related leaks may involve ice at the roof edge, a flashing defect or another route for water entry. We look at the symptoms before proposing a repair, ice dam removal or heat cables. Keep a record of the conditions and take indoor photos, but avoid attempts to remove roof ice yourself.

Can you check a skylight when assessing my Dunnville roof?

Yes, mention any dampness or staining around the skylight when arranging the assessment. We can review the surrounding roof and flashing junctions to help identify where water is entering. Repairing those connections may be appropriate, while some units need replacement. Velux skylights are available, with the recommendation based on the unit and the condition of the adjoining roof.
