How do you approach a roof beside Mitchell's historic downtown?

We start with the roof's condition and the details that affect its appearance, such as eaves, visible flashing and nearby masonry. Tell us about any known heritage designation or municipal direction. What should I check when eavestroughs overflow during rain in Mitchell?

From the ground, note where water spills over and whether a downspout appears blocked or disconnected. Leaves can collect at outlets, while sagging sections may hold water instead of draining. We can inspect the gutter slope, joints and discharge route. New eavestroughs or a targeted repair may help, depending on the condition of the existing system.
