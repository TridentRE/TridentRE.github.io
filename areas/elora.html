Elora homeowner questions

What should I consider when choosing a roof for my Elora home?

Start with the roof's slope, existing construction and the appearance you want to maintain. Ventilation, drainage and future maintenance also shape the options. We can compare suitable coverings and explain the connections that need attention during replacement. For a protected building, confirm any material or appearance requirements early so they can be included in the plan.

How do you approach flashing beside an Elora stone wall?

We assess the joint between the roof and masonry rather than treating a surface seal as the whole repair. The condition of existing flashing and the surrounding materials helps define the work. If the property is protected, the proposed detail also needs to respect the applicable approvals. We explain the limits of the roofing scope before proceeding.

Can a roof replacement include the skylight and eavestroughs?

It can. Raising those items at the first visit lets us examine how the skylight, roof covering and drainage connect. A skylight may need replacement, revised flashing or no work at all, depending on its condition. We can include Velux options and seamless eavestrough work in the proposal while keeping each part of the scope clear.
