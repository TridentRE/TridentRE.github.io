Baden homeowner questions

Does living near Castle Kilbride mean my roof has heritage restrictions?

Heritage requirements depend on your property's status and the changes proposed. Before ordering a new covering, confirm any applicable requirements with Wilmot. We can set out the roofing materials, flashing details and related trim work in a clear scope. Having those details ready helps you consider appearance and weather protection together during the review.

Can a Baden roof replacement leave the existing siding in place?

Often the siding can remain, but the roof-to-wall junction needs a careful look. Flashing may extend behind the cladding, and worn material can complicate a sound repair. We assess those connections before setting the scope. If a small area must be opened or replaced, we explain why instead of treating it as unrelated work.

What should I compare besides shingle colour for a Wilmot home?

Compare the complete scope: removal of old materials, roof deck review, underlayment, ventilation, flashing and disposal. Ask how skylights, chimneys and eaves will be handled where present. We can discuss IKO and GAF shingle options alongside those details. A colour choice matters visually, but it does not describe how the roof will manage water.
