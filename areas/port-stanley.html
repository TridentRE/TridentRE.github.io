Port Stanley homeowner questions

What will you check when a Port Stanley roof starts leaking?

We look for worn roofing, failed flashing, blocked drainage and damage around roof penetrations. Explain when the leak happens and share photos taken safely from inside or at ground level. Those details help us trace the likely water path and discuss whether a focused repair will address it or more extensive roof work is needed.

Should a skylight be checked during a Port Stanley reroof?

It is worth reviewing its condition and flashing before the new roof is installed. Keeping a sound skylight may be possible, while replacement can make sense where the unit or surrounding details have deteriorated. We install Velux skylights and can include the relevant work in the roof scope so the sequence is clear.

Can gutter problems be reviewed during a roof visit?

Yes, mention any overflow, dripping behind the gutter or water collecting beside the building when you book. At your Port Stanley property, we can review roof-edge details, fascia, gutter alignment and downspout routing together. That helps distinguish a blocked outlet from damaged components and shows which repairs would benefit from being coordinated with roof work.
