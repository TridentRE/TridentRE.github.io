Eavestrough Contractor in Brantford

Seamless aluminum eavestroughs, Alu-Rex gutter guards, and drainage design for Brantford homeowners.

A Brantford eavestrough project starts by following water from the roof edge to the downspout discharge. Seamless eavestroughs need suitable slope, secure support and downspouts that discharge to an appropriate location. An overflowing gutter may have a blockage or a fitting problem, so replacement is not the first assumption.

Trident inspects eavestroughs alongside fascia, soffit and roof edges. We can install seamless runs and gutter guards, discuss outlet locations and identify visible deterioration before new material goes on. Where exterior appearance is subject to heritage requirements, confirm those details before changing profiles or finishes. We explain ongoing maintenance as part of the plan, including checks for debris at outlets even when gutter guards are fitted.
