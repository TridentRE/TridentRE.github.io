Roofing Contractor in Lucan

Residential roof repair, replacement, and inspection for Lucan homeowners — IKO architectural systems built for Ontario weather.

A roof in Lucan's growing residential community should be assessed by its condition, not simply by when the neighbourhood was built. Missing shingles may need a targeted repair, while repeated leaks can point to wider problems with flashing, ventilation or the roof covering. Trident Roofing & Exteriors checks those details before recommending repairs or replacement. We explain what we find so you can understand which work is urgent and which items can be planned.

For replacement projects, we offer IKO and GAF shingles as well as metal roofing, with flat roofing available where the roof design calls for it. The work includes looking at edges, penetrations and the connections to eavestroughs or skylights. Lucan's commercial properties can also request roofing assessments. If water is coming inside, contact us with photographs taken safely from indoors or the ground. Emergency leak help includes some after-hours availability, subject to scheduling and safe working conditions. Never climb onto a wet or icy roof to investigate.
