Roofing Contractor in Caledonia

Residential roof repair, replacement, and inspection for Caledonia homeowners — IKO architectural systems built for Ontario weather.

A Caledonia roof inspection should answer a practical question: is the problem limited to one detail, or is the covering reaching the point where replacement makes more sense? Trident Roofing & Exteriors examines shingles, flashings, roof penetrations and drainage edges before setting out options. A ceiling stain does not necessarily sit directly below the entry point. Photos of the stain and notes about when it appears help us decide where to begin tracing the leak.

We handle shingle repairs and replacement with IKO or GAF products, along with metal roofing, flat roofs and commercial roofing. The scope can include related fascia, eavestrough or Velux skylight work where the roof meets another exterior component. For winter leaks, we assess the symptoms before discussing ice dam removal or heat cables. Some after-hours leak calls can be accommodated when availability and safe access allow. Contact our London-based team with the property location and a description of the immediate concern.
