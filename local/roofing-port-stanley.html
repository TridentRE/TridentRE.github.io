Roofing Contractor in Port Stanley

Residential roof repair, replacement, and inspection for Port Stanley homeowners — IKO architectural systems built for Ontario weather.

A roof in Port Stanley needs a clear plan for shedding water, especially where several roof planes meet. On a property exposed toward Lake Erie, the assessment also includes a close look for loose materials and damaged edge details. Trident starts by checking the covering, flashings, penetrations and roof edges. We discuss whether damage is confined to one detail or whether broader wear makes replacement more sensible. That distinction helps keep the proposed work tied to evidence from your building.

Our roofing services cover IKO and GAF shingles, metal roofs and flat roofing, including commercial applications. We review ventilation and the condition of the underlying surface as part of replacement planning. If a skylight is part of the roof, its flashing and condition belong in that discussion too. We can coordinate Velux skylight work with the surrounding roof installation. At completion, the roof edge and eavestrough connection should form a practical water path, not leave drainage as a separate problem for another visit.
