Westlake Royal insulated siding and complete exterior cladding for Guelph homes — built for Ontario weather.

On a Guelph exterior, the junction between siding and masonry deserves as much thought as the siding colour. We check how new cladding will meet windows, doors, rooflines and existing wall finishes before laying out the scope. Where a property is designated or within a heritage district, confirm the applicable guidance before changing visible cladding or trim.

Trident offers Westlake Royal and Royal Building Solutions siding options, along with Green Metal steel siding. We can include soffit, fascia and capping so the surrounding details are not left unresolved. Our assessment looks for loose panels, damaged trim and signs of water entering behind the finish. We explain repair limits, replacement options and the preparation needed for the particular wall, rather than assuming all elevations need identical work.
