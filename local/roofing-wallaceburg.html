Residential roof repair, replacement, and inspection for Wallaceburg homeowners — IKO architectural systems built for Ontario weather.

A roof problem above a Wallaceburg shop can raise different questions from a leak over a bedroom. Access, roof slope, drainage and the rooms below all affect the repair plan. We trace the likely entry points, including flashings, penetrations, shingle damage and roof edges, then explain which details need attention and how the proposed repair addresses them.
