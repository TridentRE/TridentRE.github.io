Roofing Costs in Ontario FAQ

Straight answers about roof repair and replacement costs, quotes, financing, and what affects pricing in Ontario.

How much does a roof replacement cost in Ontario?

Most residential replacements in our service area range roughly $8,000–$18,000+ depending on roof size, pitch, material, deck condition, and ventilation work. Trident provides itemized quotes after a free inspection — not phone estimates.

Is a repair cheaper than a full replacement?

Often yes for localized damage on a roof under 15 years old. Repeated repairs on an aging system usually cost more over 2–3 years than one proper replacement. We recommend what actually makes financial sense.

What affects the price of a roofing quote?

Square footage, number of layers to remove, deck repairs, chimney and skylight flashing, ventilation upgrades, material tier, and accessibility. Hidden deck rot is the most common surprise — we inspect before quoting.

Do you offer free quotes?

Yes. Inspections and consultations are free with no obligation. You receive photos, a clear scope, and pricing before any work begins.

Do you require a deposit?

Standard projects typically require a deposit to schedule materials and crew. Payment terms are outlined in your written proposal before work starts.
