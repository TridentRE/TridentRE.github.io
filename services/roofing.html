Residential Roofing Systems

Engineered roof repair and replacement for Ontario homes — built to outlast standard contractors and protect your biggest investment.

Trident Roofing & Exteriors specializes in complete residential roofing — from targeted repairs to full tear-offs and replacements. We serve London, Kitchener-Waterloo, St. Thomas, Woodstock, and communities across South and Central Ontario.

What we install

  • IKO architectural shingle systems
  • Synthetic underlayment & ice-and-water shield
  • 6-nail wind-rated fastening patterns
  • Ridge vent & attic ventilation integration
  • Flashing, valleys & drip edge engineering
  • Flat roofing membranes (see flat roofing)

Roofing services

  • Free roof inspections & honest evaluations
  • Leak repair & emergency tarping
  • Full roof replacement
  • Storm & hail damage assessment
  • Insurance claim documentation
  • Soffit, fascia & metal capping

The Trident difference

Most contractors treat a roof as isolated shingles. We engineer roofing as part of a complete exterior system — ventilation, drainage, flashing, and siding designed to work together. Our installations use premium materials and methods intended to last 50%+ longer than commodity work.
